What I've Built

Live Project
InvenTrack
Business Inventory & Sales Management Platform
View Live

InvenTrack is a full-stack, web-based Inventory Management System — a centralized digital platform enabling small and medium-sized businesses to manage their complete inventory lifecycle. From adding products and tracking stock to processing sales, generating professional invoices, and analyzing business performance through a dedicated reports module.

Key Features
Role-Based Access — Three distinct roles (Admin, Manager, Staff) with dedicated dashboards and strictly enforced permissions.
Real-Time Stock Management — Every confirmed sale instantly deducts stock via PHP/SQL and triggers low-stock alerts on the dashboard.
Automated Invoice Generation — Unique invoice numbers via PHP uniqid(), itemized bills with quantities and grand totals — printable from browser.
Modular Architecture — Centralized includes/ directory, normalized MySQL database with foreign key constraints throughout.
